Educational Takeaway for Classrooms

  • Evolving Capabilities: Generative AI models are improving daily. They can effortlessly produce high-fidelity imagery, videos, original music, speech, and written essays.
  • Detection Skills: Human brains are finding it increasingly difficult to detect synthetic media. Relying purely on "looks" or "vibe" is no longer foolproof.
  • Critical Verification: Learn to look for metadata, verify sources, inspect original publications, and ask: "Who stands to benefit from me believing this media?"

Game Guide · Classroom Best Practices

Game Instructions

1. Welcome Screen: Pick solo challenge or configure classroom team play (2-4 teams).

2. Specimen Display: Listen to voice synthesis, analyze generated artwork, look closely at photography, or spot structural logic in text and computer code.

3. Group Verdict: Have teams collaborate! Once you select AI Generated or Human Created, the explanation reveals itself.

4. Quick Timers: Complete each task within the 15-second clock window to register full points.
